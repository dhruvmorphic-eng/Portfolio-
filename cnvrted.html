Learnings

learnings.md · 5 so far
001 customers

Talk to customers before you build a single thing.

Your idea is sh*t until a customer says otherwise. Nobody gives a f**k about it. Only build what the customer actually wants, and find that out by asking, not guessing.

002 shipping

Don’t delay shipping. Ever.

Shipping is where you actually learn, mostly what the customer doesn’t want. Sit on it too long and you’re just polishing a bullsh*t feature nobody asked for.

003 sales

Sales is the most important thing. Everything else comes after.

It’s all about how you sell. Even when a deal is close, know exactly who you’re selling to, and make sure the whole company comes in line, not just the one person who likes you.

004 team

Coworkers aren’t your best friends.

Be good to them, but keep it about the work. Friendship makes the hard calls harder.

005 team

No yes-men on the team.

If nobody pushes back, you’re the only one thinking. Keep people around who’ll tell you your sh*t idea is sh*t.
