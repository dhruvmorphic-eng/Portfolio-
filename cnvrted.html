Learnings

brain.md · 5 patches
diff --git a/dhruv-before-cnvrted b/dhruv-now+5 −5
@@ -1 +1 @@ customers
01−My idea is great. Build it first, find customers later.
01+Talk to customers before you build a single thing.Your idea is sh*t until a customer says otherwise. Nobody gives a f**k about it. Only build what the customer actually wants, and find that out by asking, not guessing.
@@ -2 +2 @@ shipping
02−Ship when it’s ready. Just one more feature.
02+Don’t delay shipping. Ever.Shipping is where you actually learn, mostly what the customer doesn’t want. Sit on it too long and you’re just polishing a bullsh*t feature nobody asked for.
@@ -3 +3 @@ sales
03−A great product sells itself.
03+Sales is the most important thing. Everything else comes after.It’s all about how you sell. Even when a deal is close, know exactly who you’re selling to, and make sure the whole company comes in line, not just the one person who likes you.
@@ -4 +4 @@ team
04−Build it with your best friends.
04+Coworkers aren’t your best friends.Be good to them, but keep it about the work. Friendship makes the hard calls harder.
@@ -5 +5 @@ team
05−A team that agrees is a team that works.
05+No yes-men on the team.If nobody pushes back, you’re the only one thinking. Keep people around who’ll tell you your sh*t idea is sh*t.
