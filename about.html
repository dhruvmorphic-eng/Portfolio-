Hey, I'm Dhruv.

19 · Dropout · Builder 🏏

I'm 19, and I dropped out of Manipal (MAHE) to build.

At first I figured it'd be fun — that I could just build whatever I wanted and figure the rest out along the way. Turns out it's hard. Really hard. And somehow I picked one of the toughest spaces possible to do it in. I love it.

I love taking on challenges. I love working hard. I love the messy, no-map part of making something out of nothing. That's the whole game for me.

Before all this, I played professional cricket for almost 10 years — then walked away from it because it felt like the right call. That's a whole different story.

Right now, most of my energy goes into Cnvrted — a startup I'm building to fix the way sales works. A sharper way to find real buyers, not just names. It's the thing I'm obsessed with.

I previously worked at Morphic, an AI storytelling and filmmaking platform, where I ran marketing operations and led MorphicKIDS, our YouTube channel for kids' content. Learned a ton — now it's all Cnvrted.

Outside of that, I'm a content person at heart — always building, always networking, growing on LinkedIn.

Still early. Still building. Just getting started.
